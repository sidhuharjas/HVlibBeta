---
title: Calibration
description: Measure the camera so field positions are right.
---

Distance comes from the camera's mounting geometry. A few millimeters off in height or pitch becomes inches off at range.

| Method | Meaning | Units |
|--------|---------|-------|
| `cameraHeightIn` | Floor to the lens center | inches |
| `targetHeightIn` | Floor to the element center | inches |
| `cameraPitchDeg` | Tilt above horizontal. **Negative looks down.** | degrees |
| `cameraForwardIn` | Lens ahead of robot center | inches |
| `cameraLeftIn` | Lens left of robot center | inches |
| `minConfidence` | Lowest confidence to keep | 0 to 1 |
| `maxStalenessMs` | Oldest data to keep | ms |
| `limelightPipeline` | Pipeline index from the Limelight UI | index |
| `limelightPollingRateHz` | How often the SDK reads the camera | Hz |

## Measure

- **Height.** Floor to the middle of the lens. Same for the element.
- **Pitch.** Angle gauge against the housing. Tilted 25° at the floor is `-25`.
- **Offsets.** Robot center of rotation to the lens. Forward positive, left positive.

Take the numbers from CAD, then check them on the robot.

## Frames

Robot frame: +X forward, +Y left. Field axes match Pedro Pathing; heading is radians counter-clockwise from +X.

The camera reports `tx` (positive right) and `ty` (positive up). `FieldProjector` intersects that ray with the plane at the element's height, then the robot pose moves it into the field frame. Rays that never reach that height are dropped.

## Example

```java
VisionConfig config = new VisionConfig()
        .cameraHeightIn(9.25)
        .targetHeightIn(3.0)
        .cameraPitchDeg(-25.0)
        .cameraForwardIn(4.5)
        .cameraLeftIn(-1.2)    // 1.2" right of center
        .minConfidence(0.40)
        .maxStalenessMs(150);
```
